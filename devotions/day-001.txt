Daily Devotion - January 1

Title: A New Beginning

Scripture: "Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, the new creation has come: The old has gone, the new is here!" - 2 Corinthians 5:17

Reflection:
As we begin this new year, let us remember that in Christ, we are new creations. The old has passed away, and all things have become new. This is not just a calendar change, but a spiritual reality for those who are in Christ.

Today, take a moment to reflect on what God wants to do in your life this year. What old things need to be left behind? What new things is God calling you to embrace?

Prayer:
Heavenly Father, thank you for the gift of a new year. Help us to leave behind the old and embrace the new life you have for us in Christ. Guide us each day as we seek to follow you. In Jesus' name, Amen.
